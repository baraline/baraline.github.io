## Thesis

### Time Series Classification with Shapelets: Application to Predictive Maintenance
**Type:** Ph.D. Thesis  
**Institution:** University of Orléans  
**Year:** 2022  
**Supervisors:** Christel Vrain, Wael Elloumi

**Abstract:** This thesis explores time series classification using shapelet-based methods with applications to predictive maintenance. Research areas include time series analysis, transformation and classification, anomaly detection, survival analysis, and trigger systems.

**Keywords:** Time series analysis, Shapelets, Predictive maintenance, Machine learning, Classification
